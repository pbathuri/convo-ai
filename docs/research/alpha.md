# Alpha cohort plan

**Target:** 15–25 candidates + 1 SME per persona.

## Consent

- Recording/transcript policy version stored in `Consent` table
- Block scoring without consent

## Metrics

- NPS, perceived realism (1–5), usefulness, repeat-session intent

## Recruitment

- University career centers, alumni Slack, LinkedIn DMs
- Incentive: free extended minutes during pilot
